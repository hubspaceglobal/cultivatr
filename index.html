The Business
Pathway

For those who have completed the Cultivatr process and chosen to build their own.

Two Paths Forward

After mastery, graduates choose their direction.

PATH A

Company Placement

Placed with a Cultivatr partner company or organization. Arrive with a certificate, a completed project, and the experience to hit the ground running.

Best for: those who want to join a team or enter their field of certification.
PATH B

The Business Pathway

Select the entrepreneurial track within the Cultivatr system. Build, launch, and grow your own product or service — with the full ecosystem behind you.

Best for: innovators and builders ready to create something entirely their own.
Phase 01

IDEA

Imagination leads to ideas. Ideas lead to manifestation. Plant the seed.

  • How do I choose a team?
  • How do I determine my offerings?
  • How do I structure the business?
  • How do I choose a business name?
  • How do I establish a location?
  • How do I plant the seed?
Phase 02

INTENTION

Follow through with intention. Create a plan that lets you perform at your highest level.

  • How do I create a Business Profile?
  • How do I create a sustainability strategy?
  • How do I negotiate win-win agreements?
  • How do I scope and manage projects?
  • How do I plan the harvest?
Phases 03 – 05

Attention · Manifestation · Regeneration

ATTENTION

Fertilize & weed your plants.

  • How do I register my business?
  • How do I develop my business?
  • How do I market & pitch?
  • How do I cultivate my seeds?

MANIFESTATION

Celebrate growth. Harvest the crops.

  • Produce based on customer preferences?
  • How do I negotiate contracts?
  • How do I harvest and trade?

REGENERATION

Save your seeds. Grow again.

  • Align revenue goals with demand?
  • How do I obtain loyal customers?
  • How do I regenerate and scale?
